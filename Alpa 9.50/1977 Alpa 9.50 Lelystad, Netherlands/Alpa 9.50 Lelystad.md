The Alpa 9.50, or S&S 32, or "Italian Swan", is a very attractive-looking, well-built, and excellent-sailing S&S design. Built in Italy, she features a beautifully finished accommodation both on deck and in the cabin. She is a rugged vessel that has undertaken many fine voyages at sea, where she handles exceptionally well in rough conditions.

She features a main cabin and a lockable forward cabin, providing a total of 5 berths. The heads compartment is accessible from the main cabin and includes a marine toilet and washbasin.

The placement of the steering wheel allows for a comfortable sitting or standing position in the cockpit under all conditions. The cockpit features two lazarettes, is self-draining, and the Alpa is equipped with wide, easily navigable side decks.

The vessel has been meticulously maintained every year, with no skimping on investments. Consequently, she is in superb condition both technically and cosmetically. During the winter months, the yacht has always been stored in a shed for maintenance by her owners.

Following her purchase in 1998, an extensive maintenance and replacement program was implemented, addressing and renewing the following items:

Underwater hull completely stripped; coated with a 6-layer epoxy system; and finished with a new self-polishing antifouling (International) system (1999)

Renewed gas installation with locker (1999)

Renewed gas stove with oven (1999)

Renewed Garmin GPS (1999)

Renewed boom, mainsail, and reefing gear (2000)

Renewed standing rigging (2000)

Renewed headsails including Genoa 2 and High-Aspect jib (2001)

Renewed instruments via Raymarine ST 60 log, depth, and wind indicators (2002)

Replacement of plastic bearings on the propeller shaft (2002)

Renewed batteries, switchboards, and electrical installation (2004)

Ongoing annual maintenance to the underwater hull, teak deck, winches, engine, etc.
