*Little M*

French & Webb was commissioned to build a reproduction of this 1926 design originally named *Gee Whiz*.  She is a slight enlargement of NG Herreshoff's last personal yacht *Pleasure*.  The original 34 foot yacht is still sailing under the name of *Aida*.  Construction followed the 1926 drawing using traditional steam-bent frames and double planking.  The yacht also has a centerboard, its engine mounted off center to starboard.
