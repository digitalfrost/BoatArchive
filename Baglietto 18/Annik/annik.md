Builder’s Heritage  

Founded in the 19th century, Baglietto is one of Italy’s oldest and most respected yacht builders, with a legacy spanning early wooden sailing yachts through to modern superyachts. Their early sailing yachts were custom-built with meticulous attention to detail, reflecting the highest standards of Italian craftsmanship in the early 20th century.  

Surviving Baglietto sailing yachts from the 1920s are exceptionally scarce, making examples such as ANNIK highly desirable among collectors and classic yacht enthusiasts.  

Construction & Design  

ANNIK features classic early-20th-century hull proportions, combining slender, elegant lines with balanced displacement. Built using traditional wooden boatbuilding techniques, she offers both visual refinement and a reassuring, seaworthy feel under sail. Her design reflects an era focused on sailing pleasure and elegance.
