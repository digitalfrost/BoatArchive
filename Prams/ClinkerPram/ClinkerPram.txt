This attractive little boat was constructed on the Inner Hebridean island of Tiree off Scotland’s west coast . The island has a long history of building and using traditional wooden clinker boats.

This boat has, LOA 10ft (3.05 m ) beam 4’4″(1.32 m) depth 1′ 5″ (0.44 m ) weight 130 lbs , all measurements approximate. Planking is 9mm Scottish larch on steam bent oak frames . Copper nailed with copper roves . Bow and stern transoms and knees are Sapele . Americam white oak has been used for the thwarts, providing a pleasing contrast to the darker wood . The interior of the hull received 2 coats of Jotul primer/ undercoat finished with 2 coats of International gloss white . All bright timber received 3 coats of Coovar varnish. The skeg and bilge keels have brass rubbing strips . She will carry 3 adults without difficulty.
Alastair MacLachlan, her builder , has been building, repairing and using small boats as a life long interest . This dinghy was built with care and attention to detail and as a labour of love . She awaits service with someone who will appreciate her beauty and utility. Can deliver at cost. £5,000 ono.
