### Description

Built by William Frazier of Mevagissey Cornwall in 1926. As a gentleman’s day boat.  
She has been in the family since 1972 when my father bought her from the second owner.  
From 1998 to 2006 she was laid up and professionally fully rebuilt, she was also converted to a fixed keel from the original 400lb centre plate, as so many boats of this type were. All new spars made and a slightly larger sail plan transformed her to a fast, well balanced and brave boat.  
She is maintained to a high standard and is a joy to sail on all points of sailing.  
All sails are in very good condition.  
The jib is set on a Selden 50s reefing gear, main Sail has one set of slab reefing points. The topsail is set on a aluminium spar. There is spare small jib.  
She comes with a 5 hp Mercury outboard and bracket in good order.  
Fenders  
Cockpit cover.  
A fully braked road trailer by Lizard trailers in good condition  
She is currently afloat and being sailed in Plymouth  
Reasonable offers invited.
