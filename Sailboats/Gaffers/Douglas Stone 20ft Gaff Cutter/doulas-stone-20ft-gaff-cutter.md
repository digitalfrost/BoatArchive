AMAZING DOUGLAS STONE 20FT GAFF CUTTER FOR SALE  

Built in 1939. Carvel wooden construction of Pine planking on Elm & Oak with Larch garboards. Aft cockpit, tiller steering, iron ballast keel. Teak cabin. The bulkheads are clad with mahogany tongue and groove.  

BUILDER  

Douglas Stone  

DIMENSIONS  

LOA 19'6" plus bowsprit  
LWL 19'0"  
Beam 5'10"  
Draft 3'0"  
Displacement around 2.95 tons  

1939 Douglas Stone designed and built 20ft classic gaff cutter, carvel built, sails and rigging 2010, Yanmar 1GM10 diesel, Raymarine tillerpilot, Hummingbird plotter, cockpit cover and winter cover too, restored over many years by her experienced owner, a former boat builder. Well know & much admired local boat.

REMARKS  

"Blue Hawk" was purchased by her current (ex wooden boat builder) owner in 2013. He then spent 18 months restoring her, She has been sailed on the East Coast and raced in Old Gaffers Association events including the Maldon Regatta, Swale Match & Nore Race - winning prizes in all three! She has been cruised on every river between Lowestoft and Chatham, also to the South Broads on 3 consecutive years. She is a much loved and admired boat!
