Acorn 15 rowing dinghy designed by Iain Oughtred.  Built by the first owner in 2013, he was a professional carpenter and built the boat for himself as a hobby.

Used very little by the first owner, he passed away and the boat was put into dry storage until 2020.  The current owner has kept her in dry storage and used the boat for rowing expeditions around Plymouth Sound.

Complete with a good quality road trailer and 2 pairs of oars.

An elegant rowing boat, suitable for either 1 or 2 rowers with space in the stern for a cox.

Length on deck                 15’1”

Beam                                    4’1”

### **Construction**

Clinker planked in marine plywood, lands all epoxy glue fastened.

Laminated oak stem with an oak keel and transom.

Oak floors across the centreline epoxy glued to the hull.

Oak gunwhales and rubbing strakes. 2 rowing positions built into the gunwhales. Bronze bow fairleads.

3 thwarts on douglas fir with laminated oak hanging knees.

Transom hung rudder with a yoke and tiller lines on bronze pintles.

Scrubbed wooden sole boards screwed to the floors.

Adjustable oak foot stretchers attached to the sole boards.

Marine plywood rudder with bronze hangings. Tiller quadrant with steering lines.

### **Equipment**

2 pairs of Collars spoon blade oars with leather collars

2 pairs of bronze rowlocks

Galvanised road trailer

All over fitted cover

Rudder

Mooring lines

Fenders
