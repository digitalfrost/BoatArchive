Iain Oughtred Guillemot sailing dinghy built by Tavy Wooden Boats, Devon and first launched in 2021.

Designed by Oughtred as an excellent all round dinghy with what he thought of as the perfect length making the boat capable but also easy to live with, launch and retrieve.  The boat can carry a few people, rows beautifully and sails very well indeed making the boat a real jack of all trades.

Professionally built, her plywood/epoxy construction makes her light but strong and she will not dry out when stored ashore meaning there is no need to worry about leaks when she is first launched.

A beautiful boat complete with trailer, rig and everything you need to go boating straight away.

Length              12’2”

Beam                 4’6”

Draft                  8” / 2’8”

Weight              107kg (bare hull) / 120kg (with rig and oars)

### **Construction**

Clinker planked in epoxy glued 6mm Joubert marine plywood.

Iroko stem, keel and transom.

Douglas fir gunwhales, inwhales and rubbing strakes.

Built in buoyancy tanks fore and aft.

Douglas fir thwarts.

Marine plywood centreboard case with a douglas fir capping.  Plywood centreboard raised and lowered by han

Transom hung drop plate rudder.

Painted slatted sole boards

### **Rig**

Standing lug rig on a hollow unstayed douglas fir mast.  Mast is keel stepped with iroko support at thwart level.

Douglas fir boom and lug yard.

Tan lug mainsail by Octagon Marine, Plymouth.

Running rigging falls to belay pins by the mast.

### **Equipment**

Combination road trailer/launching trolley by Trident Marine

All over cover

Pair of Holt inflatable buoyancy bags

Pair of 7’6” spoon blade oars with bronze rowlocks

Mast and spars

Mainsail

All running rigging

Centreboard

Rudder and tiller

Mast supports for transport
