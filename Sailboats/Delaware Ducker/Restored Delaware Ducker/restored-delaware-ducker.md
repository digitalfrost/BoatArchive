## ## Description

15′ Delaware Ducker, beautifully restored in 2025. This historic sporting boat design rows and sails under a double-sprit rig. 1995 Glued Lapstrake construction, featuring classic bronze hardware and custom fishtail raised oarlocks. 15-foot push pole, Shaw & Tenny oars, Lithicum sail. Includes a 2021 Trailex single-axle trailer. Located on Lake Murray, Columbia, SC
