### Description

Very competitive 1/2 tonner for sale.  

Originally a HB31 - extensively modified with complete new deck, Mills keel (the heavier mk2 version), Jeffa rudder, Raymarine instruments, Selden carbon boom, carbon spinnaker pole & custom carbon tiller. Yanmar 1GM10 (moved forwards on top the keel) with Gori prop.  

2025 work included new Selden mast and standing rigging, spray painted Durapox paint job under the waterline & Cyclops load cell fitted to the forestay. Plenty of fresh sails including a 2025 mainsail and a 2026 J1.5 jib.  

2026 IRC rating - 0.951  

2024 - IRC Scottish Champ  
2025 - 4th IRC Nationals (class 4)  
2025 - 6th 1/2 ton cup  

Ready to race on a Mersea twin axle galvanised trailer. Located @ Royal Northumberland YC but may be able to help with delivery.
