12’ Barnegat Bay Sneak Box built in 1961 by Gus Heinrichs. Made of fiberglass over white cedar, the boat was refinished in 1984 by Gus Jr. and wasn’t hunted after that. (Boat in photo is example of a sneakbox grassed in.) 
These boats are legendary in the Northeast for hunting the coastal marshes. The low profile and backrest allows you to layout easy. I was told the boat does 25mph with a 9.9hp from the old fella I purchased it from but never had a chance to even use it unfortunately. Weighs roughly 150lb. I have original oars and ice pick, too. A very cool and unique piece of duck hunting history that deserves to be back in the marsh. Title in hand. $1500
