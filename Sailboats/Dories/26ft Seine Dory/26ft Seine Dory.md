# 26’ Seine Dory

$27,800.00

Pronounced “sane”, this is the largest boat in our Banks dory line, the Seine dory is named for the large circular nets set for mackerel, herring, tuna and cod.

Seine boats were used to collect the fish from these nets and were towed to and from them. They have over 5000 lb. capacity and are great big, roomy craft that can be outfitted in any number of ways, including power by motor or sail, or the addition of a small cabin.

Included with your boat: Set of oars, tholepins and a classic dory bailer.

Option to add motor well or rigging. You can also purchase additional accessories.
